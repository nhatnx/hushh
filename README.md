Babies use distinct vocalization patterns for different physical states (e.g., hunger, pain, fatigue) due to physiological reflexes. For instance, a "hunger" cry often includes a "Neh" sound (tongue hitting the roof of the mouth), while a "sleepy" cry is more of an "Owh" (yawning reflex).
